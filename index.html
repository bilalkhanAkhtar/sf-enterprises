Questions people ask

What does the warranty cover?

Hardware faults such as motherboard, RAM, SSD, screen and keyboard for 6 months from the day you buy. Physical damage and liquid spills are not covered. Final terms are written on your bill.

Can I see the laptop before I pay?

Yes. Visit the shop, switch it on and try it. You can also ask for a video of the exact laptop on WhatsApp.

Can the RAM or storage be upgraded?

Most models allow an upgrade to 16 GB RAM or a larger SSD. Ask us for the price when you enquire.

Do you deliver outside Mumbai?

Delivery across India can be arranged for an extra charge. Ask on WhatsApp for the cost to your pin code.

Will I get a bill?

Yes, a proper GST bill with the serial number and warranty dates.
